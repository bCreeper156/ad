用户与评论政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 27 日

欢迎来到 156blog。为维护理性的交流环境，本站评论要求填写昵称与邮箱，但不会公开展示邮箱。请遵守以下规则：

1. 发言规则

  • 必填信息：发表评论需填写昵称与邮箱，二者均为必填项；网址为可选。
  • 禁止内容：严禁发布包含攻击他人、网暴、歧视、造谣、色情、暴力、恐怖主义或垃圾广告等违法违规内容的评论。
  • 友善交流：欢迎不同观点，但请基于事实和逻辑表达，对他人保持基本的尊重。

2. 人机验证

为防止机器人滥用，提交评论前需通过 GeeTest（极验） 人机验证（滑动/点选等）。该验证由 GeeTest 提供，行为数据由其服务端处理，我们不存储您的鼠标轨迹等原始行为数据，仅保留验证是否通过的结果。

3. 管理权限

  • 删除与隐藏：有权删除或隐藏违反上述规则的评论。
  • IP 日志留存：系统会在您提交评论时记录 IP 地址及 User-Agent，存储于 Cloudflare D1 数据库中。该日志随评论数据一并保存；评论被删除（用户自行删除或管理员删除）时，日志一并清除。此数据仅用于反垃圾审计及依法配合监管机关调取，不作他用。
  • 违规处理：对于严重违规者，有权删除评论并限制其后续评论提交。

4. 免责声明

  • 评论区内容仅代表评论者个人观点，不代表博主或本站立场。
  • 因网络延迟、服务器故障导致的评论提交失败，博主不承担赔偿责任。
  • 评论内容一经发布，请自行承担相应责任；博主不对评论的准确性、完整性承担连带责任。

5. 法律适用与管辖

本博客服务器部署于境外，评论数据处理遵循 GDPR 标准。鉴于互联网无国界，若您在中国大陆境内访问本站，请知悉：中国法律禁止在网络上传播违法信息。如评论内容违反中国法律，博主将依法配合相关机构进行调查与处置。

Comment Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Welcome to 156blog. To maintain a rational discussion environment, comments require a nickname and email address; your email will not be publicly displayed. Please observe the following rules:

1. Rules

  • Required Information: To post a comment, a nickname and email address are required; a website URL is optional.
  • Prohibited Content: No harassment, hate speech, defamation, pornography, violence, terrorism, or spam.
  • Friendly Communication: Different views are welcome, but please express them based on facts and logic, and treat others with basic respect.

2. Human Verification

All comments require GeeTest human verification (slide/captcha). Verification is processed by GeeTest; we do not store your raw behavioral data (e.g., mouse paths), only whether the verification passed.

3. Moderation

  • Deletion: The blog owner may delete or hide violating comments.
  • IP Logs: When you submit a comment, the system records your IP address and User-Agent, stored in a Cloudflare D1 database. This log is retained alongside the comment; when the comment is deleted (by you or the administrator), the log is cleared together. This data is used solely for anti-spam auditing and lawful cooperation with regulators.
  • Violation Handling: Severe offenders may have their comments deleted and their ability to submit further comments restricted.

4. Disclaimer

  • Comments represent the personal views of the commenter and do not represent the blog owner or this site.
  • The blog owner is not liable for failed submissions due to network issues.
  • Once posted, commenters bear responsibility for their own content; the blog owner assumes no joint liability for the accuracy or completeness of comments.

5. Governing Law

This site follows GDPR. Visitors in China must still comply with local laws; illegal content will be handled lawfully.
