用户与评论政策

最后更新：2026 年 8 月 15 日

欢迎来到 156blog。本站实行匿名评论，但匿名不代表免责。

1. 发言规则

  • 无需绑定手机号，昵称请勿冒充他人。
  • 禁止网暴、歧视、造谣、色情、暴力及垃圾广告。
  • 评论开放时间为每日 8:00 – 22:00（服务器时间）。

2. 人机验证

提交评论前需通过 Cloudflare Turnstile 验证，防止机器人滥用。

3. 管理权限

博主有权删除违规评论、留存 IP 日志（≤90 天）并封禁严重违规者。

4. 免责声明

评论仅代表用户观点，博主不承担因网络故障导致的提交失败责任。

5. 法律适用

本站遵循 GDPR；中国境内访客仍需遵守本地法律，违法内容将依法处置。

简言之：你可以匿名说话，但不能乱说话；我有责任删垃圾，但不会要你手机号。

Comment Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Welcome to 156blog. We offer anonymous commenting; anonymity does not mean immunity.

1. Rules

  • No phone number required; do not impersonate others.
  • No harassment, hate speech, defamation, pornography, violence, or spam.
  • Commenting is allowed daily from 08:00 to 22:00 (server time).

2. Human Verification

All comments require Cloudflare Turnstile verification to prevent bot abuse.

3. Moderation

The blog owner may delete comments, retain IP logs (≤90 days), and ban severe offenders.

4. Disclaimer

Comments are user‑owned. The blog owner is not liable for failed submissions due to network issues.

5. Governing Law

This site follows GDPR. Visitors in China must still comply with local laws; illegal content will be handled lawfully.

In short: you may speak anonymously, but not recklessly; spam will be removed, and your phone number will never be asked for.
