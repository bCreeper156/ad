隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 8 月 15 日

本博客（156blog）部署于境外，面向全球访客提供服务。我们高度重视你的隐私，并依据 GDPR 及《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》制定本政策。

1. 我们收集哪些数据

为反垃圾评论与保障安全，系统自动记录：评论内容、昵称、邮箱（可选）、IP 地址、User‑Agent 及 Cloudflare Turnstile 验证数据。

2. 数据用途

仅用于展示评论、拦截垃圾与运维排查。绝不用于广告画像、追踪或出售。

3. 存储与保留

评论长期保留；IP 与技术日志短期留存（≤90 天）后自动清理或匿名化。

4. 你的权利

你有权访问、更正或删除你的评论及相关数据。请联系博主行使权利。

5. 法律适用

本博客服务器位于境外，遵循 GDPR；若你位于中国境内，亦遵守 PIPL 长臂管辖，博主依法管理违法内容。

如有疑问，请通过博客渠道联系我们。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

156blog is hosted outside China and available globally. We respect your privacy and comply with GDPR and China’s PIPL.

1. Data We Collect

To prevent spam and ensure security, we automatically process: comment content, nickname, optional email, IP address, User‑Agent, and Cloudflare Turnstile verification data.

2. Purpose

Data is used solely to display comments, block spam, and maintain service stability. We never use it for ads, tracking, or selling.

3. Retention

Comments are retained until deletion is requested. IP logs are kept for a short period (≤90 days) and then purged or anonymized.

4. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your comments and related data. Contact the blog owner to exercise these rights.

5. Governing Law

The blog is hosted offshore under GDPR. If you are in China, PIPL still applies to illegal content, and the blog owner will comply with lawful requests.

For questions, contact us via the blog.
